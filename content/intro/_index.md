CoLab (Cola Laboratory, 可乐实验室) was founded on June 12, 2016. Our current research focuses on embodied intelligence in real-world environments, with emphasis on UAV, VLA, and multimodal video understanding. We also study 3D perception for autonomous systems and intelligent scheduling for satellite constellations, building AI agents that can perceive, reason, and act across air-space-ground scenarios.
